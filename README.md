### 🚀 About Me

I am a developer and visual designer passionate about turning complex ideas into accessible, engaging digital products. 

My journey started in **2020** with foundational web technologies—HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Over the years, I've evolved into modern web application development, specializing in **TypeScript, React, Vite, and Next.js**.

Beyond software development, I bridge the gap between design and code. In college, I deepened my mastery of **Figma**, crafting human-centric UI/UX designs for web and mobile platforms. My creative toolkit also extends to **video editing** and **graphic design**, allowing me to build cohesive visual narratives across media.
